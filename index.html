About Top Car Motor Sport

Your Local Auto Repair Shop

Top Car Motor Sport was founded with a simple mission: to bring personal, honest auto repair back to Tewksbury. We're tired of the big chains, the runaround, and the upselling. So we built something different.

We're a team of certified technicians who genuinely care about your vehicle. Whether it's routine maintenance or a complex diagnosis, we treat every car like it's our own. With over 2 years of combined experience, we've earned the trust of 500+ happy customers across 12 major brands. Our commitment to excellence ensures 100% customer satisfaction on every job.
